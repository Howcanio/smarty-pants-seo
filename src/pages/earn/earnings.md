This page sets out what Smarty Pants offers Mac owners today and what comes out of it. The offer can change, and nothing here is a promise of income.

## What does the intro offer pay?

Earn up to $1/day per eligible Mac during the intro offer. Check [smartypants.network](https://smartypants.network/) for the current terms.

| Term | What the live site says (8 Nov 2026) |
|---|---|
| Rate | Up to $1 a day per eligible Mac, before withdrawal fees |
| Who | The first 50 supported Apple Silicon Macs with 16 GB of memory or more, MacBooks included |
| Where | US only for now |
| Paid for | Hours the Mac is on, plugged in, online and ready, even with no job running |
| Closes | Once 50 Macs have joined, the offer closes to new Macs |

A Mac that is off, asleep, unplugged or paused part of the day earns less. Full terms are shown in the app before your Mac starts earning.

## What is the payout fee?

Payouts go through Stripe, and the fee is 3% with a $3.00 minimum. On a $30.00 withdrawal, 3% is $0.90, which is below the minimum, so the fee is $3.00 and you keep $27.00.

The Tax and Payout Policy on the [legal center](https://smartypants.network/legal) adds that earnings are platform credits, not bank deposits or guaranteed cash. A balance can be paid only after the published threshold, payee checks, reconciliation and any holds. US payees may be asked for Form W-9 and a valid taxpayer ID.

## What does it cost me?

Displayed earnings are gross. Electricity, internet and tax are your costs, and Smarty Pants does not give tax advice.
