This page sets out exactly what Smarty Pants offers Mac owners today, what comes out of it, and what it costs you to run the Mac. Every figure is dated and sourced. The offer is an intro offer, it can change, and nothing here is a promise of income.

## What does the intro offer pay?

Up to $1 a day per idle Mac, until 50 computers join. That is the offer [smartypants.network](https://smartypants.network/) listed on 8 November 2026; check the live site for the current terms.

The terms, from the [owner FAQ](https://smartypants.network/humans) and home page:

| Term | What the live site says (8 Nov 2026) |
|---|---|
| Rate | Up to $1 a day per idle Mac, before withdrawal fees |
| Who | The first 50 supported Apple Silicon Macs with 16 GB of memory or more, MacBooks included |
| Where | US only for now |
| Paid for | Hours the Mac is on, plugged in, online and ready, even with no job running |
| Monthly | "About $30.00 a month if your Mac is on, plugged in and ready all day" |
| Closes | Once 50 Macs have joined, the offer closes to new Macs |

"Up to" matters. You are paid only for the hours the Mac is ready, so a Mac that is off, asleep, unplugged or paused part of the day earns less. Full terms are shown in the app before your Mac starts earning.

## What is the payout fee?

Payouts go through Stripe, and the fee is 3% with a $3.00 minimum. The live home page gives one example: "Withdraw it once a month and you keep $27.00 after the payout fee."

The math: 3% of $30.00 is $0.90, which is below the $3.00 minimum, so the fee is $3.00 and you keep $27.00.

The Tax and Payout Policy on the [legal center](https://smartypants.network/legal) adds that earnings are platform credits, not bank deposits or guaranteed cash. A balance can be paid only after the published threshold, payee checks, reconciliation and any holds. US payees may be asked for Form W-9 and a valid taxpayer ID.

## Are electricity and taxes deducted?

No. "Displayed earnings are gross: they do not estimate or deduct electricity costs or tax obligations." Electricity and internet are your costs.

The owner FAQ says the tax-reporting process, "including when a 1099 may be issued, is not final," and owners will get written terms before live payouts. Smarty Pants does not give tax advice, so ask your own adviser.

## What happens after the first 50 Macs?

Smarty Pants has not published a rate. It says pay will depend on "what your Mac can do: its memory, its speed, and how often it's on," and "we tell you in the app before your pay changes."

We don't estimate it. The [Node Operator Agreement](https://smartypants.network/legal) says no demand, utilization, minimum work, availability, earnings or profit is promised.

## What does the electricity cost?

For a 16 GB M4 Mac mini at the US average price, between about $0.52 and $8.50 a month, depending on how hard the Mac works. Here is the math with Apple's own power figures and EIA's average price.

**Inputs:**

- Price: 18.16 cents per kWh, the US average residential price for August 2026 in the [EIA Electricity Monthly Update](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/update/end-use.php) (released 23 October 2026). EIA calculates this as average revenue per kWh. Your own rate may be higher or lower; check your bill.
- Power: from [Apple's Mac mini power consumption page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/103253) (published 21 September 2026), measured at the wall. "Idle" means only Finder open with default power settings. "Max" is Apple's maximum figure.

**Formula:** watts × 24 hours × 30 days ÷ 1,000 = kWh a month. kWh × $0.1816 = cost a month.

| Mac mini (Apple's test config) | Power | kWh a month | Electricity a month |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024, [M4](/macs/m4/), 16 GB | 4 W idle | 4 × 720 ÷ 1,000 = 2.88 | 2.88 × $0.1816 = about $0.52 |
| 2024, M4, 16 GB | 65 W max | 65 × 720 ÷ 1,000 = 46.8 | 46.8 × $0.1816 = about $8.50 |
| [M5 Pro](/macs/m5-pro/), 64 GB | 6 W idle | 6 × 720 ÷ 1,000 = 4.32 | 4.32 × $0.1816 = about $0.78 |
| M5 Pro, 64 GB | 145 W max | 145 × 720 ÷ 1,000 = 104.4 | 104.4 × $0.1816 = about $18.96 |

A real month sits somewhere between the idle and max rows. Running a model uses more power than sitting at the Finder, and the app keeps the Mac awake while earning is on, but a ready Mac is not always running a job. We can't tell you where in that range your Mac will land.

Read it this way: against the intro offer of about $30 a month (or $27 after the payout fee), a 16 GB M4 Mac mini's electricity is between about $0.52 and $8.50 a month at the US average price. A higher-power Mac at max draw costs more. Internet, wear and tax are on top, and not counted here.

## Is it worth it?

It can be, for a Mac that is already on, plugged in and idle much of the day, in the US, while the intro offer is open. It is a weaker fit if your electricity is expensive, the Mac is a laptop you carry around, or you would need to buy a Mac just for this. Pay after the intro offer is not published.
