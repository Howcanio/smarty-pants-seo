## Using five of these at once?

Most teams running agents stitch together a model API, a batch service, sandboxes, a dev VM, Mac CI and a worker host. Smarty Pants puts models, embeddings and batches behind one key and one balance today, adds workspaces for agents (invite only for now), and has CI, scheduled jobs and Docker builds coming. All of it is free on your Group's Macs. Read [one account instead of five](/compare/one-account-for-ai-agents/).

## What is the short version?

Smarty Pants is the most complete option for teams running AI agents: open models, workspaces for agents and free use of the Macs you already own, under one account, priced at about 95% of the cheapest hosted seller we check. Mac owners are paid in dollars for being available, not only for work served. The others are steadier and bigger: dedicated servers, GPUs, more context and compliance paperwork. A common setup is Smarty Pants for work that is fine on someone else's Mac, or on your own, with a hosted provider as the fallback.
